Simply Trace

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 22, 2026
Simply Trace is designed for children. We take privacy seriously and collect minimal data. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information from our users.

1. Information We Collect

Simply Trace does not collect any personal information. Specifically:

  • We do not require account creation or login.
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, or any contact information.
  • We do not collect location data.
  • We do not use cookies or tracking technologies.
  • We do not collect device identifiers for advertising purposes.

2. Photos and Camera

Simply Trace allows users to select photos from their device library for tracing purposes. These photos:

  • Are processed entirely on the device.
  • Are never uploaded to any server.
  • Are never shared with third parties.
  • Remain under the user's full control at all times.

3. In-App Purchases

Simply Trace offers optional subscriptions and a lifetime purchase through Apple's App Store. All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple. We do not have access to any payment information, credit card details, or billing addresses.

4. Children's Privacy

Simply Trace is designed to be safe for children of all ages. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
  • We do not include behavioral advertising.
  • We do not include social media integrations.
  • We do not include links to external websites that are not age-appropriate.

5. Data Storage

All app data, including tracing progress and collected reward cards, is stored locally on the user's device. No data is transmitted to external servers.

6. Third-Party Services

Simply Trace does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The only third-party service involved is Apple's StoreKit for processing in-app purchases, which is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer any user data to third parties. Since we do not collect data, there is nothing to share.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
